How it works

Three steps, about five seconds

  1. 01
    Drop in a lead's website and LinkedIn

    Five fields: name, email, phone, company site, LinkedIn URL.

  2. 02
    Icebreak scores the lead against your ICP

    Each criterion is checked against a specific page or section, and the snippet is kept.

  3. 03
    You get a fit score, the reasoning and an opener

    Written for that company, referencing something they actually said. Copy it and send.

The difference

Your ICP, your rules

A score is only useful if you set the terms. You write the criteria in your own words, mark each one as a must-have, a nice-to-have or a disqualifier, weight it, and say where to look for it.

Change a criterion and you see what it does to a real lead before you save. When a rep asks why a lead scored 42, the answer is a list they helped write.
